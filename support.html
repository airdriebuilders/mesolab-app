MesoLab support

Help for the MesoLab apps (Android, iPhone) and the web app.

Contact us

Email mesolabsltd@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. You can also use the Feedback tab in the app to report a problem, suggest an exercise or a better demo video. Add your email there if you'd like a reply.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone only. There's no account and no cloud copy, so export a backup now and then: Settings → Export (the app opens the share sheet so you can save the file to Files, Drive or email). Settings → Import restores it.

Moving to a new phone? Export a backup on the old phone and import it on the new one. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may also restore the app's data.

Why can't I open a later day? Days unlock in order. Finish (or start) the next day first; Today always shows which one it is.

A demo video won't play. Videos need an internet connection. If one still won't play, tap "Open in YouTube" and tell us via the Feedback tab so we can replace it.

How does MesoLab choose my sets and weights? Volume starts near your minimum effective volume and changes week to week from your post-session feedback (pump, soreness, workload), within each muscle's maximum recoverable volume. Weights go up in small steps (rounded to 0.5 kg or 1 lb) while reps-in-reserve targets get harder, followed by a deload.

Can I change kg/lb? Each mesocycle has its own unit, chosen when you create it.
